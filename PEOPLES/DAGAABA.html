In DAGAABA subcluster areas, long histories of migration and warfare make clans vulnerable to slave raiding until British colonial government in Ghana implants chieftainships. Historically, the origins of Dagaaba people has been a complex mystery to tackle, probably related to a schism with Dagbamba.

In LoDagaa areas, the local rise of Islam in the late twentieth century alters religious and musical life, contributing to a decline in xylophone-based practices associated with animism.
