Together with other ancient empires (Ancient Ghana, Mali, Songhai, Ashanti), Great Benin of Edo people flourished in the part of the present-day Nigeria from 1200s to 1800s, and participated in long-distance trade of gold, ivory and salt, accumulating wealth through this trade.

Musicians of Benin Kingdom have a presence as cultural representations through the museum display of bronze plaques showing horn players and drummers.
