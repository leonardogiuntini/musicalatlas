(Poro)

Before about 1400, groups in this area, particularly those in Liberia, Sierra Leone and west Côte d’Ivoire, felt little influence from the savanna empires of Ghana and Mali; this isolation allowed the development of small, widely scattered states. With the disintegration of the Mali Empire in the 1400s, Malinke traders and warriors moved from the savanna into the kola plantations of the forest, bringing merchandise and Islam, affecting areas in and around Liberia.
