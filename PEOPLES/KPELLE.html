migrations into the area known as Liberia occurred between the 1400s and the 1800s, bringing Kpelle musical and social institutions into Liberian territory. Poro and Sande secret societies are established as vehicles for transmitting culture. Woi-meni-pele epic genre. Kpelle timekeepers concepts of time (months named after sound qualities; “year” as a “fence” enclosing social action) are linked to how performances are structured. proverb “if you build a town and there’s no drummer in it, then it’s not a town”.
