Africa is a huge continent.

- From the 16th century, the Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade carries enslaved Africans and their traditional musics to the Americas;

- After slavery abolition in the 19th century, repatriated freed slaves and their descendants’ musics (jazz, blues, calypso, reggae) influence neo-traditional and contemporary African art forms.

- Early 1980s shift to musicassettes: the spread of domestic cassette players revolutionizes the creation, performance and distribution of music; piracy emerges via widespread “duplicator” businesses and international companies reduce investment; major studios begin coasting on existing equipment.
